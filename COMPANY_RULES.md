## Human handoff

Queue the conversation immediately when the customer asks for a human, employee, call, meeting, pricing, detailed quotation, contract, invoice or company visit; also when information is missing or an answer cannot be reliable.

An explicit, high-confidence handoff request must enter the human queue before optional information collection. The customer must never be required to finish a form before being queued.

When the AI is available, notify the customer naturally that the request is with the team. A meeting request is a request for follow-up, not a confirmed calendar booking.

While waiting, the AI may collect only missing details, one question per incoming customer turn: name; company or business field; preferred contact time; relevant project/service details. The WhatsApp sender number is already known: use it as contact unless the customer supplies another. Never repeatedly demand the same field; accept refusal and leave handoff active. Information collection is optional for the customer and never removes the conversation from the queue.

Once the employee takes over, the AI must not send acknowledgements, questions or normal replies. The employee explicitly returns the conversation to the bot when appropriate. No automatic release at closing time or after inactivity.

On AI failure, store the error and queue for the employee. No fabricated AI response. Any fixed outage notice requires separately approved bilingual wording; absent that wording, the failure is visible in the inbox and no automatic outage text is sent.
